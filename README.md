## The Platform: Enterprise Intelligence Layer

Most enterprise engineering teams spend thousands of hours every year hunting across Confluence, Jira, Slack, GitHub, PagerDuty, ServiceNow, and a dozen other systems — context-switching instead of building.

I designed and built the **Enterprise Intelligence Layer** from zero to production to solve that exactly:

- **Unified ingestion** — a single framework that digests entire corporate ecosystems (wikis, tickets, incidents, code, runbooks, org data)
- **Shared intelligence** — one knowledge graph that serves developers, SREs, architects, security, and leadership through role-aware AI surfaces
- **Autonomous action** — goes beyond search; triggers workflows, surfaces anomalies, and accelerates decisions at scale
- **AI-native from day one** — not retrofitted AI on legacy tooling; purpose-built for LLM-driven retrieval and reasoning

Adopted across multiple engineering organizations. Built, owned, and shipped end-to-end.
